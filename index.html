About

About Rent Management System

Rent Management System (RMS) is an academic project developed as part of our Software Engineering course. It is designed to help property owners and tenants manage rental operations in a structured and efficient way.

Owners can manage their properties, tenants, rent collection, and complaints, while tenants can view their rent details, make payments, and submit complaints. RMS aims to simplify everyday rental management tasks through a clean and user-friendly interface.
